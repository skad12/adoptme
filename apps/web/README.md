# AdoptMe Web

The AdoptMe web experience helps visitors explore responsible pet listings, adoption opportunities, animal welfare campaigns, and care-focused seller profiles.

## Pet Experiences

- Browse dogs, cats, birds, adoption listings, exchanges, and responsible breeding listings.
- Review pet profiles with health notes, vaccination details, microchip status, temperament, and care documents.
- Create listings that explain the animal's needs, background, training, and ideal home.
- Support rescue and shelter campaigns through donation pages.
- Guide families toward safer handovers and better long-term pet care.

## Content Priorities

- Animal welfare first
- Clear health and care records
- Responsible breeding transparency
- Adoption readiness
- Safer communication between owners, adopters, shelters, and donors
